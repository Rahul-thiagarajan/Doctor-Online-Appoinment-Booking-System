About GENCARE


GENCARE was founded in 1998 by Dr. Arjun Mehta, a visionary healthcare professional with a mission to make quality healthcare accessible to everyone. What started as a small clinic has now grown into a multi-speciality hospital with state-of-the-art facilities and a team of expert doctors across various disciplines.


Our philosophy is built on compassion, trust, and excellence in patient care. We believe in combining advanced medical technologies with personalized treatment, ensuring every patient receives the attention they deserve.


With more than two decades of experience, GENCARE continues to lead the way in innovative healthcare solutions, research, and community health initiatives.
